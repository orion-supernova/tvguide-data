Get help or report a problem

Missing channel, wrong listing, or a bug? Open an issue and include your country and the channel name.

Frequently asked

How do I change the country? Settings (gear icon in Guide) → Country.

Reminders don't arrive. Allow notifications for TV Guide in iOS Settings → Notifications.

How often are listings updated? Several times a day, about a week ahead where the source provides it.
